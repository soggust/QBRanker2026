Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

Season Ranker ranks players from public stats. You can use the rankings without an account. An account is optional and lets you save things. This page says what we keep, who can see it, and how to remove it.

Without an account

  • Analytics. Google Analytics (through Firebase) records page views and general device and location information (country, browser) so we can see which pages are used. It isn't tied to your account.
  • Your browser. Your sliders, filters and display settings are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device only.
  • Pictures and odds. Team logos, headshots and game odds load from ESPN and the leagues' public sites, so your browser contacts them as it would any image.

With an account

Accounts run on Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). We keep:

  • Your sign-in. Your email address and the sign-in methods you use (Google, Facebook, or an email and password; passwords are handled by Firebase and never seen by us). Signing in with Google or Facebook shares your name, email and profile picture with us.
  • Your profile. Your username, display name, picture (your provider's, one you upload, or a team logo), and your privacy choices.
  • What you save. Presets, saved lists and the lists you submit to the Community with the votes you cast, pinned comparisons in Track Players, your friends and friend requests, and, where it's open, your play-money wallet and bets with their record.

Play money is never real money. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers. A spam check (Google reCAPTCHA, through Firebase App Check) may look at your device and browser to tell people from bots.

Who sees what

  • Your username, display name and picture are public: anyone can find your profile by username.
  • Your lists, presets, tracker and bets are shown to everyone, to friends only, or to no one, as you choose in Settings → Privacy (and per list).
  • Community submissions and the votes on them are public. Leaderboards show only those whose bet history is public.
  • Your email address is never shown to anyone.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your data while you have an account. Delete the account and it's removed, as the Data Deletion page explains.

Children

Season Ranker isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep their data.

Data Deletion

Last updated {{ updated }}

You can delete your Season Ranker account and its data yourself, at any time, whichever way you signed in (Google, Facebook, or email and password).

Delete it yourself

  1. Sign in, open the menu under your name (top right) and choose Settings.
  2. In Profile, choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm: type your username, and your password (or sign in again with Google or Facebook).

That's it: you're signed out and the account is gone. It can't be undone.

What's deleted

  • Your sign-in (Firebase Authentication account) and your profile; your username is free for someone else.
  • Your presets, saved lists, Community submissions, the votes on them and the votes you cast.
  • Your pinned comparisons (Track Players) and your friends and friend requests, on both sides.
  • Your play-money wallet and your settled bets, right away.
  • Bets still open when you delete (a game not yet final) and your betting record are removed by the hourly job that settles bets, within about an hour; your row leaves the leaderboards at the same time.

Not deleted: Google Analytics page views, which aren't linked to your account (Google keeps them under its own retention), and settings saved in your browser (clear them with your browser's site data).
